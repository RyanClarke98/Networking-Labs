Lab 2 — Router-on-a-Stick

Objective:
Configure inter-VLAN routing using Router-on-a-Stick (ROAS).

Configuration:

R1 G0/0 configured as an 802.1Q trunk
G0/0.10 → VLAN 10 → 192.168.10.254/24
G0/0.20 → VLAN 20 → 192.168.20.254/24
PCs configured with the appropriate default gateway

Result:
Successful communication between VLAN 10 and VLAN 20.

Key Learning:
A router can use subinterfaces with 802.1Q encapsulation to route traffic between multiple VLANs over a single physical interface.
